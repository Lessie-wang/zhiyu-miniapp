# 新增4种情绪的AI图片生成提示词

## 设计风格要求
- 莫兰迪色系，柔和温暖
- 抽象几何风格，不要具象的人脸
- 简洁优雅，符合知愈小程序的整体设计语言
- 尺寸：1024x1024px

---

## 1. 幸福 (happiness/bliss)

**iconType:** `happiness`
**主色调:** `#FFE5CC` (温暖的杏色)
**辅色调:** `#FFF5E8`

**AI提示词：**
```
A minimalist abstract illustration representing "happiness and bliss" emotion, featuring soft warm apricot and cream colors in Morandi palette (#FFE5CC, #FFF5E8). Gentle flowing curves forming a heart-like shape with radiating soft light, surrounded by small floating circles suggesting joy bubbles. Clean geometric composition with subtle gradients. Warm, peaceful, and deeply content feeling. No human faces. Soft shadows, dreamy atmosphere. 1024x1024px.
```

## 使用说明

1. 将以上提示词输入到 AI 图片生成工具（如 Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion）
2. 生成后保存为 PNG 格式
3. 文件命名：
   - `happiness.png`
   - `lonely.png`
   - `embarrassed.png`
   - `proud.png`
4. 放置到目录：`D:\Obsidian\10-Projects\知愈 FeelingMosaic\知愈 微信小程序\images\emotion-icons\`
